Data & Account Deletion

We believe in giving you full control over your data. You can manage, update, or permanently delete your information at any time. This page explains how.

As required by the Google Play Store, this page provides instructions for managing and deleting your account data, both from within the app and if you no longer have the app installed.

How You Can Manage Your Data

You don't have to delete your entire account to manage your data. You have control over your profile information directly within the app:

  • Updating Your Information: When you edit your profile and provide new information (like a new profile name, bio, or email), the old information is permanently overwritten and deleted from our active database.
  • Updating Your Photos: When you upload a new profile picture or swipe photo, the old photo it replaces is permanently deleted.
  • Deleting Your Photos: You can delete any of your photos (including your main profile picture or swipe photos) at any time from the "Edit Profile" screen, even without replacing them.

How to Delete Your Account (From Inside the App)

The easiest and fastest way to permanently delete your account is directly through the app:

  1. Log in to the NETD4TE app using your unique username and password.
  2. Navigate to the "My Profile" screen (usually the icon at the bottom-right of the navigation bar).
  3. On your profile screen, tap the menu icon (☰ or ⋮) in the top-right corner to open the side menu.
  4. In the menu, scroll down and tap on the "Delete Account" option.
  5. A confirmation pop-up will appear. To finalize, tap the "Confirm" button.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When you confirm account deletion, the process is **immediate and irreversible.**

  • Permanent Deletion: Your account and all associated personal data are permanently deleted from our active databases. There is no 30-day grace period or recovery option.
  • All Data is Removed: This includes:
    • Your profile (name, username, email, DOB, gender, country)
    • Your authentication details
    • Your profile picture and all swipe photos
    • Your bio and Instagram username
    • All match history, visitor history, and pending requests
    • All chat conversations and messages
    • Any remaining in-app currency (Gold, Diamonds)
  • Subscriptions: Deleting your app account **does not** automatically cancel active subscriptions (Pro, Pro Max) billed through the Google Play Store. You must manage and cancel these separately through your Google Play account settings.

How to Request Deletion (If You Can't Access the App)

If you have uninstalled the app and can no longer access the in-app deletion button, you can still request deletion by contacting our support team.

  1. Send an email to support@netd4te.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. To verify your identity, you **must** send the email from the email address associated with your NETD4TE account and include your **unique username** in the body of the email.

Once we verify your ownership, we will process the permanent deletion of your account and notify you via email.

Data Retention Policy

While your active personal data is deleted immediately, we may retain certain non-personal, anonymized, or aggregated data for analytical purposes to improve our services. Additionally, records related to financial transactions (like subscription purchases made via Google Play) may be retained for the period required by law for financial auditing and compliance. For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.

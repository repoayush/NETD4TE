Account Deletion Instructions

We value your privacy and make it straightforward to delete your NETD4TE account and associated data directly within the app. Please follow these steps:

Steps to Delete Your Account:

  1. Log in to the NETD4TE app using your unique username and password.
  2. Navigate to the **"My Profile"** screen. You can usually find this by tapping your profile picture icon located at the bottom right corner of the main screen's navigation bar.
  3. On your "My Profile" screen, look for the **menu icon** (often represented by three horizontal lines or dots ☰/⋮) in the top right corner. Tap this icon to open the side drawer menu.
  4. In the menu drawer that slides out, scroll down to the bottom.
  5. Tap on the **"Delete Account"** option.
  6. A confirmation dialog box will appear asking if you are sure you want to delete your account. Read the confirmation message carefully.
  7. To proceed with deletion, tap the **"Confirm"** button. If you change your mind, tap "Cancel".

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Upon confirming the deletion:

  • Your account will be **immediately and permanently deleted**. There is no 30-day grace period.
  • All your personal profile information, including your profile name, username, bio, date of birth, gender, country, and email address will be removed from our active databases.
  • Your profile picture and all uploaded swipe photos will be permanently deleted.
  • Your match history, request history (sent and received), and visitor history will be deleted.
  • All your chat messages and conversations will be permanently deleted.
  • Any remaining Gold or Diamond balances will be forfeited.
  • Your connection to any active subscriptions through Google Play will need to be managed separately through your Google Play account settings.

Data Retention

While your active user data is deleted immediately upon request, please note that we may retain certain anonymized or aggregated data for analytical purposes to improve our services. Additionally, records related to financial transactions (like subscription purchases) may be kept for a period required by law for auditing and compliance purposes. For more details, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues deleting your account through the app, please contact our support team at support@netd4te.com for assistance.

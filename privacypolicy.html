Privacy Policy for NETD4TE

Effective Date: October 28, 2025

Last Updated: October 28, 2025

NETD4TE is developed and operated by AYUSH (operating as BLOODRAVEN), an individual developer based in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh, India. Your privacy is our top priority, and we are committed to being transparent about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the NETD4TE mobile application (the "App" or "Service"), how we use that information, and the choices you have regarding your data. By using NETD4TE, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Our Privacy-First Philosophy

Before diving into the details, we want to emphasize our core commitments to protecting your privacy:

  • We Do Not Track Your Precise Location. We never request or use your device's GPS coordinates. Our app only asks for your country, and our "Nearby Explore" feature uses location keywords that you control—not real-time tracking.
  • We Do Not Access Your Camera. We will never ask for permission to access your device's camera.
  • We Have Minimal Access to Your Photos. We only access the specific photos you choose to upload. The Android system restricts us to only those selected images—we cannot see or access any other photos on your device.
  • We Do Not Sell Your Data. We will never sell your personal information to third parties.
  • No Email Marketing. We only send emails for security and account verification purposes—never for promotional or marketing campaigns.

3. Information We Collect

To provide you with the best dating experience, we collect several types of information. Here's exactly what we collect and why:

A. Information You Provide During Account Creation

When you register for NETD4TE, you provide the following information:

  • Profile Picture: One main profile photo selected from your device's gallery.
  • Three Swipe Photos: Three additional photos for the swipe interface, also selected from your gallery.
  • Profile Name: A display name visible to other users (can be any name—real, nickname, or made-up).
  • Username: A unique identifier for your account (can be any username you choose).
  • Email Address: Used for account verification, security alerts, password resets, and two-factor authentication. We verify your email with a one-time password (OTP) before account creation.
  • Bio: An optional description about yourself (maximum 500 characters).
  • Country: Your country of residence (used to show you other users from the same country). You can update this anytime.
  • Instagram Username: Optional plain text field (not linked to Meta/Instagram APIs—just displayed as text if you want matches to find you on Instagram).
  • Date of Birth: Required to verify you are 18 years or older. We calculate and display your age to other users.
  • Gender: Male, Female, or Other (used to show you profiles of the opposite gender based on your selection).
  • Password: Encrypted and stored securely using bcrypt hashing (minimum 6 characters).

B. Information Collected Through Your Use of the App

As you use NETD4TE, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Swipe History: Records of profiles you've swiped left on (to avoid showing them again until you've seen all available users).
  • Match Data: Information about users you've matched with.
  • Request Data: Connection requests you've sent or received (normal and "introvert" requests).
  • Chat Messages: All messages you send and receive are encrypted using AES-256-CBC encryption and stored securely. We automatically limit chat history to the most recent 114 messages per conversation to save database storage. When the 115th message is sent, the oldest message is permanently deleted.
  • Profile Visits: If you are not a verified user, the app tracks when other non-verified users view your profile. This information is hidden until you unlock it using in-app currency (diamonds). Verified users' visits are not tracked, and their unlocks are free.
  • Blocked and Muted Users: Lists of users you've blocked or muted.
  • Virtual Currency Balance: Your gold and diamond balances, daily reward streaks, and last claim timestamps.
  • Notification Settings: Your preferences for receiving push notifications for messages, requests, matches, profile visits, etc.
  • Online Status: Whether you are currently active in the app (visible to your matches).
  • Last Active Timestamp: When you were last active in the app.
  • Ghost Mode Status: Whether you've enabled Ghost Mode to hide your profile from the explore screen.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Status: Whether you've enabled 2FA for login.
  • Location Keyword: An optional keyword you can set for the "Nearby Explore" feature (e.g., "Downtown," "University Area")—not GPS coordinates.
  • Account Status: Whether your account has received any temporary or permanent bans, including reasons and admin notes.
  • Emoji Reaction Frequency: Which emojis you use most often (to suggest frequently used reactions).

C. Device Permissions We Request

To enable certain features, NETD4TE requests the following permissions from your Android device:

  • INTERNET: Required for all network communication with our servers.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Allows you to select photos from your gallery. On Android 13+, we use READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, which only grants access to the specific images you choose—not your entire photo library.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used for temporarily saving images during the upload process (required on older Android versions).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the app to send you push notifications (Android 13+ only). You can disable this in your device settings.
  • VIBRATE: Enables your device to vibrate when you receive a notification.
  • BILLING: Required for in-app purchases through Google Play Billing (subscriptions and virtual currency purchases).

Note: We never request camera permission. You can only upload photos that already exist on your device.

D. Information Collected Automatically

Like most mobile apps, we automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app configuration (collected via Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Log Data: When the app crashes or encounters an error, we collect error logs, device IP address (anonymized), device name, and the time/date of the error through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Analytics Data: We use Firebase Analytics and Google Analytics only for crash reporting—not for tracking user behavior or activity patterns.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Create and Manage Your Account: Your profile information is displayed to other users to facilitate matches and connections.
  • To Enable Communication: Chat messages are encrypted and stored to allow you to communicate with your matches.
  • To Personalize Your Experience: We use your country and gender preferences to show you relevant profiles.
  • To Process In-App Purchases: We use Google Play Billing to handle payments for premium subscriptions (Pro and Pro Max plans) and virtual currency purchases. We do not store your payment information.
  • To Send Important Emails: We use the Brevo email service to send you:
    • Registration verification OTPs
    • Password reset codes and links
    • Email change verification OTPs
    • Two-factor authentication codes
    • Account security alerts (e.g., temporary or permanent ban notifications)
    • We never send promotional or marketing emails.
  • To Send Push Notifications: With your permission, we use OneSignal to send you notifications about new messages, match requests, profile visits, and other app activities. You can control which notifications you receive in the app's settings.
  • To Display Ads: We use Google AdMob to show non-personalized advertisements. AdMob may collect certain device information for ad delivery, but we do not provide them with your personal profile data.
  • To Reward Ad Viewing: When you watch a rewarded video ad, AdMob sends a server-side verification to our backend to confirm you watched the ad, and we then grant you the reward (gold or diamonds).
  • To Improve the App: Crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics help us identify and fix bugs to improve the app's stability.
  • To Enforce Our Policies: We monitor reports from users and take action (including temporary or permanent bans) against accounts that violate our Community Guidelines or Terms of Service.
  • To Provide Customer Support: When you contact us with questions or issues, we use your information to respond and resolve your concerns.

7. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect it:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/SSL.
  • Encryption at Rest: User data stored on our MongoDB servers is encrypted at rest.
  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before being stored in the database. We never store passwords in plain text.
  • Message Encryption: All chat messages are encrypted using AES-256-CBC encryption before being saved to the database. Only the sender and receiver can read the decrypted messages.
  • Secure Image Storage: Profile pictures and swipe images are stored in Firebase Storage with access controls to prevent unauthorized access.

However, please note: No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

A. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account Data: We retain your account information for as long as your account is active.
  • Chat Messages: We automatically limit chat history to the most recent 114 messages per conversation. When the 115th message is sent, the oldest message is permanently deleted. This helps us save database storage while maintaining recent conversation history.
  • Deleted Accounts: When you delete your account, all of your data is immediately and permanently removed from our servers with no recovery period. This includes:
    • Your profile information
    • All photos (deleted from Firebase Storage)
    • All chat messages
    • All match and request data
    • All reports you've submitted
    • All other associated data

Important: Account deletion is irreversible. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. Matched users will not see a "This user deleted their account" message—you will simply disappear from their matches with no trace.

B. What You Can and Cannot Delete

  • You CAN delete: Profile pictures, swipe images, bio, Instagram username, and other optional profile fields from the Edit Profile screen.
  • You CANNOT delete individually: Username, email address, or password. If you want to remove these, you must delete your entire account.
  • You CANNOT change: Your date of birth/age after account creation.

C. No Data Export Feature

NETD4TE does not currently offer a feature to download or export your data. If you need a copy of your information for any reason, please contact us at privacy@netd4te.com, and we will assist you.
